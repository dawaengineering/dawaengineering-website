About DAWA

Engineering solutions for industrial customers.

DAWA Engineering Co., Ltd. provides engineering, procurement, construction, maintenance and technical services for industrial customers. We support customers from site survey and engineering design to material supply, installation, testing, commissioning and after-sales service.

Our focus is practical engineering, safe execution, fast response and flexible coordination with qualified business partners, contractors and manufacturers.
